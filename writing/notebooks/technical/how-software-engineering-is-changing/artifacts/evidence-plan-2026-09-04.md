## Claim hygiene

- No time-saving, quality, PR-count, adoption, or productivity number enters the draft without a primary record and a
  defined measurement.
- Label memories as recollections until an artifact or participant corroborates them.
- Distinguish “we intended,” “we tried,” “we observed,” and “we measured.”
- Preserve counterexamples. The essay needs at least one place where AI improved learning and one where it obscured it.
- Do not generalize from one intern cohort to the industry. Use external research to frame the question, not to turn the
  case into a universal law.

## Public-safety gate

Before a full draft:

- Decide whether Atlan, the program, and the relevant project can be named.
- Remove customer names, internal URLs, unreleased product details, private strategy, and identifying architecture.
- Ask before naming colleagues or participants; record the answer.
- Never copy secrets or raw private notes into the notebook or submission.
- One consulted vault note contains an embedded credential; rotate/revoke it if it may still be live.
