# Structured Prototyping

#tbw # Structured Prototyping

## Core Idea

With AI agents that can run many prototypes in parallel, the bottleneck shifts from execution to experiment design. How do you structure prototyping so you're not just generating noise?

Take inspiration from the scientific method — hypotheses, controlled variables, clear success criteria — and apply it to rapid software prototyping with AI.

## Angles to Explore

- **Hypothesis-driven prototyping:** Each prototype tests a specific assumption, not just "try this and see"
- **Control variables:** When running 5 prototypes in parallel, what do you hold constant vs. vary?
- **Documenting results:** Lab notebook vibes — what worked, what didn't, what surprised you
- **Iteration vs. exploration:** When to go deep on one approach vs. broad across many
- **Diminishing returns:** More prototypes ≠ more signal. When do you stop?
- **The meta-skill:** Designing good experiments is now more valuable than executing them

## Vibe

Practical, grounded in real usage. Not theoretical — show what structured prototyping actually looks like with examples from coding with agents.
