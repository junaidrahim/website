# Managing Open Loops with your AI Assistant

- I was talking to Janaki at the meetup and then I uttered a feature I'd want for glitch that really stuck with me.
- I said that I with it could look at my entire weekly chat sessions and suggest blog post ideas from that
- A further iteration of that idea would be this idea of managing all of your open loops with your AI assistant, where each open loop is like a email thread.
- Nikunj Kothari recently tweeted about this
  - https://x.com/nikunj/status/1983314179043012852?s=20

Most of my explorations with solving for a personal assistant have been upstream from the obsidian UX, where you have a graph of free-form notes taking shapes closer to what are the things you keep thinking about. And then you want assistance / intelligence which is heavily grounded in that context.

- So this new assistant would have two major abstractions -- Notes and Loops.
- Notes are your good old markdown notes
- Loops are more synonymous to projects, but they are deeply open ended, and with a chat first interface. Like you just keep saying what you want and the loop keeps self organizing until you declare it closed.
- Having too many open loops is a sure shot way to feel burnt out.
- And the whole point of the system is to increase loop closure percentage and sort of prevent loop explosion.
