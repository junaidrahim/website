# LLMs and Continuous Writing

So I have been thinking about how an LLM can generate some side content about curiousity directions.

If you ask fundamentally, what is a blog ?

It's public writing to either say something or prove a point. Earlier writing used to be a way to change opinion. Now it's an instrument to showcase curiosity trajectories.

Like blogging about a specific topic signals that I am interested in that particular topic

Like one end of the spectrum is running a white label substack which has a extremely clear value prop. I write this substack to talk about profiling crime in a city.

Personal blogs are a little flexible in their value props to the reader because they sort of reflect general curiosity trajectories of people and you can use that to gauge vibe fits etc.

This idea can be a separate blog all together on like "Documenting your curiosity trajectories"

One extreme of this idea usually is the digital garden. All notes are public by default.
