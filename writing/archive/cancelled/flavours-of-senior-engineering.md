# Flavours of Senior Engineering

A reflection on what kind of senior engineer to become — surfaced during the Dec 2025 career inflection point.

> "I need to spend some time to write down this set of confusions about promotion/craft/what flavour of senior engineering I want to grow into." — Dec 17, 2025

## The Tension

Several competing flavours observed at Atlan:

1. **The Architect** — system design, RFCs, technical vision. Writes docs, thinks in abstractions, designs for the long term. Risk: becomes disconnected from code.

2. **The Executor** — ships relentlessly, closes PRs, hits deadlines. Deep in the code. Risk: becomes a "feature factory" without influence on direction.

3. **The Platform Builder** — builds tools, frameworks, and infrastructure that other engineers use. Multiplies output. Risk: work is invisible to business stakeholders.

4. **The Evangelist** — writes blogs, gives talks, mentors interns, runs hackathons. Builds `concepts/proof-of-work-artifacts` and external visibility. Risk: not enough "real work" in the eyes of management.

## The Personal Conflict

The things Junaid enjoys most (writing, building developer tools like `projects/kryptonite-engineering-knowledge-platform`, speaking, mentoring) map to the Evangelist/Platform Builder flavours. But promotions at Atlan seemed to reward the Executor flavour:

> "Watching all these people get promoted has stung me a bit" — Apr 14, 2025

> "Work without an expression of your unique individuality quickly becomes very mechanical and stagnant." — Oct 15, 2025

## Resolution

The move to `personal/dbt-labs-career-move` was partly an answer to this question — choosing an environment where the Evangelist/Platform Builder flavour is more aligned with the company's needs.
