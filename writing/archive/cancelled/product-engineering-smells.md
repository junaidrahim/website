# Product Engineering Smells

## 3 Product Engineering Smells

[https://twitter.com/AjeyGore/status/1601107899375550464](https://twitter.com/AjeyGore/status/1601107899375550464)

**🪄Smart summary:**
The biggest product engineering smells are long-lived branches, code with no tests, and no automation. These smells lead to longer merge times, no trust in change, and longer delivery times.

What are the biggest product engineering smells apart from

1. Long-lived branches > leading to long merge times
2. Code with no test > leading to no-trust in change, thus longer QA cycles
3. No Automation at > build, infrastructure, and testing - Leading to a long delivery time
