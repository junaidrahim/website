👋🏻 Hi, I’m Junaid.

I’m a software engineer based in Bengaluru, India. I’m interested in distributed systems, data platform infrastructure,
and in general anything that requires high developer empathy.

I am currently part of the CTO's office at [Fivetran](https://www.fivetran.com/) working on applied AI. My focus is on
helping AI systems utilize more of the business data and domain knowledge that exist in an enterprise, this includes
building a lot of agentic systems and training different kinds of semantic models.

Previously, I spent about five years at [Atlan](https://atlan.com/). I joined as an intern and witnessed the journey of
building the metadata sync and data lineage platforms.

After leaving Atlan, I co-founded [Clu](https://clucode.com/), with my friend [Rohan](https://x.com/rohankmr414),
exploring a context layer implementation for teams that build software with coding agents. You can read more about Clu
[here](https://clucode.com/).

Writing is another way I work through ideas. It helps me slow down and stay with a question long enough to understand
it. I write about systems I have worked on, tools I am building, and questions I linger on for too long.

I have spoken at ArgoCon Europe in Amsterdam, alongside KubeCon 2023, about packaging and distributing Argo Workflows.
At DuckCon #5 in Seattle, I spoke about building scalable data pipelines with DuckDB. You can find the slides,
recordings, and proposals in my [talk archive](https://junaid.foo/talks/).

This website is a collection of all of my musings, I find the idea of having a personal corner of the internet pretty
romantic. You can read my [technical posts](https://junaid.foo/posts/), explore the projects behind them, or browse my
[bookshelf](https://junaid.foo/shelf/).

I also write more personal essays on my substack that I have pretentiously named
[Undertones](https://substack.com/@junaidrahim). I'd probably try making a living as a writer if I become part of the
permanent underclass.

Away from work, I enjoy reading in Cubbon Park, cycling around Bengaluru, and partake in being the occasional street
photographer. Some of those photos are on [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/@junaidrahxm).
